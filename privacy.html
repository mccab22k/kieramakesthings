Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all software applications, websites, projects, prototypes, and services published or maintained by Kiera Makes Things ("Services"), including current and future products.

Information We Collect

None of the apps have accounts, sign-ups, ads, or analytics. The things you create in them (cities, trips, chores, pet photos, and preferences) are saved on your own device and are not sent to us.

The one exception is in-app purchases. Apps that offer a paid upgrade use Apple's App Store to take payment and RevenueCat to keep track of what you've bought. When you open one of these apps, RevenueCat assigns a random, anonymous ID to that install and records your purchase history: which products you bought, when, the price, and the currency. Your country or region and your device type may be recorded too. This information is used only to unlock features you paid for and to restore them on a new device. It is not linked to your name or email address, not used for advertising, and not used to track you across other apps or websites. We never receive your payment card details; Apple handles all payments.

If an app starts collecting anything beyond this, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Should a future Service collect more data, it may include information you voluntarily provide (account information, preferences, tasks, schedules, uploaded content, feedback, and communications) and technical information such as device type, operating system, browser information, crash logs, diagnostic information, and usage analytics.

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand basic site traffic, such as page views, referrers, browser information, and general location data. This helps evaluate which projects people visit without adding advertising trackers.
