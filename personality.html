Personality Systems

Personality is rarely as tidy as a single label. These are experiments in looking at patterns, dimensions, and how we relate to ourselves and other people.

Attachment Styles

Two reflection questionnaires built around the anxious, secure, and avoidant patterns described in Attached, using original statements:

  • My attachment patterns: 42 statements about how you handle closeness, distance, and reassurance.
  • A partner’s patterns: 33 behaviors you have actually observed, with room to say “not enough information”.

Results show every pattern’s score side by side, so overlapping tendencies stay visible.
